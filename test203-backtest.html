How the replay matches TEST 203

At each eligible price index, it calculates the latest 8 price changes and the preceding 20-change volatility baseline. It requires at least 6 of 8 changes in one direction, net directional efficiency of at least 65%, and recent average absolute movement between 0.60× and 1.80× the baseline.

A qualifying CALL wins if the price 5 ticks later is higher; a PUT wins if it is lower; an equal exit is a tie. It does not open overlapping simulated contracts: after an entry at tick i, the next signal check is tick i+6.

Important limitation: past proposal payouts, execution latency, and exact live quote availability are not contained in historical ticks. The backtest assumes the live payout filter passes every signal. A win rate below 58%, fewer than 1,000 trades, or non-positive estimated P/L means TEST 203 is NOT cleared for live DEMO testing yet. Even a pass only justifies further DEMO research, never real-money use.
