Mamba-inspired 1-minute scalper

This is a mechanical reconstruction of the public rules described by FXSnipers for MambaFx's 1-minute strategy: M5 EMA(50) direction + M1 breakout of the previous 10 completed candles. It is not an official MambaFx bot.

Important: the original spot-FX method uses a swing-based stop-loss and a 1:3 take-profit. Deriv Rise/Fall contracts do not provide that same exit structure. This is a separate 1-minute contract adaptation, not an exact copy. DEMO only.

Strategy notes

BUY: last completed M5 close above M5 EMA(50), then current price breaks above the highest high of the previous 10 completed M1 candles.

SELL: last completed M5 close below M5 EMA(50), then current price breaks below the lowest low of the previous 10 completed M1 candles.

Signals require adequate candle history and fresh quotes. The bot skips if the trend is unclear, the contract duration is rejected, the payout is not greater than stake, or a contract is already open. Forward-test and backtest before considering any real-money use.
