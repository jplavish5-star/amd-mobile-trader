Exact replay rules

At each eligible tick i, the last three prices must be strictly rising (CALL) or strictly falling (PUT). Entry is the price at tick i. The simulated contract settles at tick i+5. CALL wins only if the settlement price is higher; PUT wins only if it is lower; equal prices are ties with zero simulated P/L.

Ticks i+1 through i+5 are collected but no new trade is opened. The next signal check is tick i+6, using the latest three ticks, including ticks observed during the previous contract.

The historical replay cannot reproduce the live payout filter, changing quotes, execution delays, or actual contract settlement prices. Estimated P/L is an illustration, not a Deriv statement or a prediction of future returns.
